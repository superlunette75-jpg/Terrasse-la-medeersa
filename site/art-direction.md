# La Terrasse de la Medersa: artistic direction

Confirmed with the owner's representative, October 2026. Keep every later change consistent with it.

- **Feeling:** sunlit, calm, authentic. A rooftop terrace in an old foundouk of the medina,
  with the generous Moroccan welcome that tourists come for.
- **Preset:** Terrasse Médina, pushed warmer and spicier.
- **Palette:** cream tadelakt `#F3E9DA` (bg), `#FBF5EC` (surface), paprika terracotta
  `#B04A24` (accent), saffron ochre `#C98A2B` (small touches only), ink `#2B1E16`,
  muted `#6F5A4A`, line `#E3D3BE`.
- **Type:** Cormorant Garamond (titles, dish names), Manrope (text, buttons), Pinyon Script
  only for the word "Bienvenue / Welcome / Bienvenido" (echoes the paper menu).
- **Signature:** the heart-and-diamond corner ornaments of the paper menu, redrawn as fine
  terracotta line ornaments (hero frame and section dividers).
- **Photos:** generated with Higgsfield. House style: natural late-afternoon golden
  sunlight, traditional terracotta tagines and hand-painted ceramics, cream tadelakt table,
  mint tea glass out of focus, 45°, warm saffron/paprika grade. Venue photos are the real
  foundouk, enhanced (light only).
- **Sound:** short Moroccan instrument notes synthesised in the browser: oud pluck on add,
  darbouka "tek" on remove, small oud motif on send. Mute toggle, remembered.
- **App feel:** installable PWA, standalone, offline cache, instant load, QR code for tables.
- **Languages:** FR (default), EN, ES.
- **Ordering:** takeaway only, orders on WhatsApp, read in French.
